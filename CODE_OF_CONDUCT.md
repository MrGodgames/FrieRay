# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the community
- showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers through GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
